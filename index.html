Pasteur Lion Uploader

What this app does

Pasteur Lion Uploader is a personal desktop tool built by Hervé Nsimba ("Bizz"). It lets him send his own original Christian motivation videos in French ("Pasteur Lion") from his computer directly to his own TikTok account (@herve.lut) using the official TikTok Content Posting API (Direct Post).

How it works

  1. Connect his TikTok account through the official TikTok OAuth login.
  2. Choose one of his original video files on his computer.
  3. Publish it to his account with Direct Post.

Permissions requested (scopes)

user.info.basic
video.upload
video.publish

OAuth tokens are stored locally on the user's computer only. No data is collected, shared, or sold.

Contact: via TikTok @herve.lut
