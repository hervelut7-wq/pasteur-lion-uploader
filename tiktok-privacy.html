Privacy Policy — Pasteur Lion Uploader

Last updated: October 3, 2026

1. What this app does

Pasteur Lion Uploader is a personal tool built by its sole user to publish one short vertical video per day to his own TikTok account (@herve.lut). It is not offered to the public.

2. Data we access

Through the TikTok Login Kit and Content Posting API, the app accesses only:

  • Basic TikTok profile information (username and profile ID) — scope user.info.basic — to confirm the connected account.
  • Video upload and publishing capability — scopes video.upload and video.publish — to post the user's own videos.

3. How data is used

The accessed data is used exclusively to publish the user's own videos to his own TikTok account. No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

4. Data storage

OAuth access tokens are stored locally on the user's own computer and are never transmitted to any third-party server.
